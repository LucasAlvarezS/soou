# Code of conduct

soou follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, keep criticism about the work and not
the person, and remember that people contribute in their spare time and in
different languages. Spanish and English are both welcome here.

Harassment, insults, and publishing someone's private information are not
acceptable, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere the project is
represented.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes you uncomfortable, report it privately through
GitHub (**Security → Report a vulnerability** works for this too, or contact the
maintainer through their GitHub profile). Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may remove comments, commits or contributions that break this
code, and may block people who repeatedly do so.
